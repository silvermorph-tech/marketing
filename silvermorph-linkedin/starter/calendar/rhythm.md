# Posting rhythm (proposal v0, edit freely)

Principle: events (launch, award, signed partner, approved customer) always jump the queue. Everything else is scheduled from the backlog so the page never goes quiet.

## Weekly: 2 posts (Tue + Thu), upgrade to 3 once the backlog is deep

| Slot | Default | Rotates with |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | Industry Insight (audience 1: operators; every 4th insight carries an investor lens) | Product slice when something shipped |
| Thu | Product slice | Partnership intro, Team, Customer story (only if approved) |

## Monthly mix of 8 posts

| Category | Per month | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Industry Insight | 3 | Operator pain points, ageing-economy logic |
| Product (existing features, sliced) | 2 to 3 | `backlog.md` |
| Partnership | 1 (up to 2 early on) | 5 partners staggered, one intro every 2 to 4 weeks |
| Team | 1 | Events, hires, awards |
| Customer story | 0 to 1 | Only after sign-off |

## How one existing feature becomes several posts (slicing)

Each live feature yields up to four posts, spread over different weeks so they never run back to back:

1. **The pain**: one concrete moment in a care home that the feature removes (Insight or Product)
2. **How it works**: capability-level walkthrough with one clean English visual (Product)
3. **The outcome**: a number or an approved quote, once we have one (Customer story)
4. **The origin or the partner angle**: why we built it, or who it was built with (Team or Partnership)

## Investor lens (audience 2)

About one post in four adds: market structure, why small operators are underserved, traction stated only with approved numbers, founder judgement. Same format, same visuals, no separate "investor" look.

## Rules

- Never two posts of the same category on consecutive posting days.
- A partner announcement waits for the partner's go-ahead date.
- Anything with an UNVERIFIED fact is blocked until the facts bank says OK.
