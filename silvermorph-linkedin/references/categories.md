# Silvermorph LinkedIn: 5 category specs (v2, owner-approved direction 2026-10-14)

Every post is exactly one category. Audience order for all: (1) aged-care and home-care people, (2) investors, (3) other industries. English only. Voice core and banned patterns: `writing-rules.md`. Layouts: `design-guide.md`.

Each spec: **Job** (what the post does) · **Reader gets** · **Trigger** · **Boundaries** · **Structure** (beats) · **Length** · **Hashtags** (2 to 4, always `#SilvermorphTech` plus others from the list only) · **Layouts** (pool, rotate) · **Gates** (must be true before publishing).

## 1. Team News
- **Job:** show the people behind the product are credible and warm.
- **Reader gets:** a team that knows care and tech first-hand.
- **Trigger:** event happened, award, new member, milestone (backlog T-xx).
- **Boundaries:** team achievements and culture only. No product pitch, no industry POV, no salaries or departures, nobody embarrassed.
- **Structure:** 1 fact opener (what, where, when) · 2 why we were there · 3 the person and one concrete thing they did or said · 4 what it tells about the team, one line, no adjectives · 5 thanks and one specific question.
- **Length:** 400 to 800 chars.
- **Hashtags:** `#TeamSilvermorph` `#LifeAtSilvermorph` `#HomeCare` · situational `#Hiring` `#NewHire` `#AwardWinner` `#Gerontech`
- **Layouts:** 1A event recap (photo, date chip, lower-third) · 1B person spotlight (name, arch portrait)
- **Gates:** real photo supplied · every named person has consent on file · one real quote or detail from the user (never invented).

## 2. Product Update
- **Job:** make one feature feel obviously useful in 20 seconds.
- **Reader gets:** which daily chore gets easier, and how.
- **Trigger:** feature ships, or a backlog slice P-xx is due.
- **Boundaries:** one feature per post. Root cause and mechanism, never a feature list. No roadmap, no competitors, no unverified performance numbers.
- **Structure:** 1 the root cause of the pain (why it is slow) · 2 what Ezzy does about that cause · 3 how it works in 2 or 3 capability-level lines · 4 what changes for the person · 5 where to try it (link in first comment) and one question.
- **Length:** 500 to 900 chars.
- **Hashtags:** `#ProductUpdate` `#NewFeature` `#HomeCare` · situational `#AgeTech` `#SaaS`
- **Layouts:** 2A chat on phone (voice note to schedule) · 2B match card (giant number, ranked result)
- **Gates:** screen shown is real or clearly stamped DEMO in the post copy ("in our demo") · every number in facts bank with a basis · no compliance wording beyond the approved text.

## 3. Industry Insight
- **Job:** teach one way of seeing a problem. Longest copy.
- **Reader gets:** a root cause they can reuse, and remembers who explained it.
- **Trigger:** a genuine position exists. Max 2 per week. Not tied to company events.
- **Boundaries:** high-level, defensible takes. No how-tos, no tutorials, no product pitch (one closing line at most), no unsourced market statistics.
- **Structure:** 1 thesis (a claim, never a question) · 2 the structural reason, first principles · 3 why more effort at the symptom does not fix it · 4 the lever · 5 us in one line · 6 one specific question that invites disagreement. Investor-lens posts add the market logic in beat 4 or 5.
- **Length:** 800 to 1,400 chars.
- **Hashtags:** `#HomeCare` `#AgeTech` · pick 1 or 2: `#HealthTech` `#AIAgents` `#FutureOfWork` `#EnterpriseAI`
- **Layouts:** 3A thesis typography (big type, key phrase block) · 3B diagram (tangled thread vs straight line). May also be a carousel later.
- **Gates:** the owner has accepted the position (backlog marks it) · no invented statistics · passes the "could a competitor post this unchanged?" test.

## 4. Customer Story
- **Job:** turn one customer's result into proof.
- **Reader gets:** believes it works for someone like them.
- **Trigger:** customer has signed off on content.
- **Boundaries:** the customer's outcome is the story, not a feature list. No unapproved names, logos, quotes or numbers.
- **Structure:** 1 result opener (the number or the quote) · 2 who they are · 3 the situation before · 4 what changed, 2 or 3 lines · 5 their own words, verbatim · 6 thanks and one question.
- **Length:** 600 to 1,000 chars.
- **Hashtags:** `#CustomerSuccess` `#CaseStudy` `#HomeCare`
- **Layouts:** 4A quote card (giant quote mark) · 4B number first (old struck through, new giant)
- **Gates:** written sign-off from the person and the organisation on file · the number's measurement method and period on file · the quote matches the approved wording exactly.

## 5. Partnership
- **Job:** announce a collaboration so that both sides matter, and say what starts and when.
- **Reader gets:** what the combination does that neither side does alone, and that it is real (a dated first step).
- **Trigger:** signed, live integration, or announced joint project. Time it with the partner.
- **Boundaries:** equal weight to the partner. No letters of intent or verbal deals, no undisclosed terms, no partner roadmap.
- **Voice and structure (owner's model: the SenseAI post, 2026-10-08):**
  1. **Title line:** `Silvermorph Tech × [Partner] | [the gap this closes, as a short phrase]`
  2. **The field observation:** open with something concrete that happens in care and goes unseen or unsolved. It must be true to what the team has seen. This is the hook and the reason the post is not empty.
  3. **Why it matters** to care teams, one sentence.
  4. **The announcement:** "With this in mind, we are pleased to announce a collaboration with [Partner], bringing together [our strength] and [their strength] to explore [what]."
  5. **The first concrete step:** where it starts, with whom, how long (for example a feasibility study, then a pilot with a stated number of households).
  6. **Forward line:** we look forward to working with the [Partner] team and sharing what we learn.
  7. **Partner link** on its own line, then the hashtags.
- **Length:** 700 to 1,100 chars.
- **Hashtags (5 to 6, the owner's practice):** `#SilvermorphTech` `#[Partner]` `#AgeTech` `#ElderlyCare` `#Gerontech` `#CareInnovation`
- **Layout:** 5A reference lockup, square 1200 x 1200 (partner logo, divider and ×, Silvermorph logo, corner stripes, category tag kept). 5B ecosystem orbit is only for an overview post with every named partner's agreement.
- **Gates:** the first step stated with real numbers or "to be announced", and no unconfirmed integration claimed. Consent is presumed: the owner starts a partnership post only after the partner agreed to the announcement, the date and the use of its logo, so never ask about it. Find the partner's LinkedIn page to tag in the composer (@ search) instead of asking for the link.

## Cross-category rules
- One post, one category. Choose by whose story it is.
- Facts come from `inputs/00-facts/facts-bank.md` (status SITE or OK) or from the user in chat. UNVERIFIED facts are blocked.
- Never two posts of the same category on consecutive posting days.
