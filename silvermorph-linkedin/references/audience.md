# Audience, channel, language (locked by owner, 2026-10-14)

- **Channel:** Silvermorph company page only. Voice is "we" / "Silvermorph". Founder appears in third person unless a founder-account skill is added later.
- **Language:** English only. No Chinese characters in copy or on posters (partner names may keep their own branding).
- **Audience priority**
  1. Aged-care industry people: care home operators first, then clinicians, coordinators, associations, regulators.
  2. Investors: ageing-economy, healthtech, impact investors. Show market logic, traction and judgement.
  3. Other industries: only through insight posts, never at the cost of 1 and 2.
- **Implication per category:** every post must be readable by a care home manager in 20 seconds. Investor value comes from evidence (numbers, named deployments, structural arguments), never from jargon or hype.
- **Cadence:** event-driven for launches and news; the rest is scheduled from `calendar/` (see `calendar/rhythm.md`).
